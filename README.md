# Hi, I'm Salvatore Bruno 👋

**Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Engineering** at the **University of Salerno**, working at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Embedded Systems, and Digital Health**.

My research focuses on the development of **non-invasive biosignal acquisition and processing systems** and on **machine learning / deep learning algorithms** for extracting physiologically and clinically relevant information from biomedical signals.

I enjoy building complete intelligent systems across the full stack — from **signal acquisition and data processing**, to **ML model development and embedded inference**, up to **mobile applications and user-facing software**.
